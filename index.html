ABOUT

Creative ideas.
Technical execution.

I'm Jacob Jones, a lighting designer and programmer based in the St. Louis area.

I started behind the scenes in high school theatre and quickly found my place behind a lighting console. Since then, my work has expanded across theatrical productions, concerts, dance, touring production and large-scale pixel installations.

I enjoy the point where creativity and technology meet: taking an idea, building the system behind it, and turning it into something an audience can feel.
